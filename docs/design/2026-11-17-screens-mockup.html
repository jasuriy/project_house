Project House screens

The five main screens in the chosen system: professional blue + green, Inter, Minimalism. Desktop is drawn at 1280 px, tablet at 768 px (held upright), phone at 390 px. A tablet held sideways, about 1024 px, uses the desktop layout with smaller margins. Phone screens show the first screen only (390 x 844), because that screen decides whether someone keeps reading.

All people, companies and the student's projects here are made up (Aziz Rahimov, Dilnoza Karimova, Nova Systems). The feed uses projects from our temporary demo data, described in our own words. Images are drawn placeholders. Uzbek interface text is a first draft.
